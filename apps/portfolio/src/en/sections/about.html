About me

Hi, I’m Kevin. I design to connect ideas and people.

I’m Kevin Garzón, a senior graphic designer and art director based in Bogotá, Colombia. Over more than six years I’ve worked with agencies, brands and direct clients, creating identities, campaigns and digital experiences. I start by understanding the problem: when the questions are clear, the visual direction lands with more strength.

Photography is how I train my eye. It taught me to observe calmly, wait for the light and notice the details others miss. I bring that same attention to every brand, website and campaign.

UX/UI

I design digital experiences that make sense at first glance. I organize the content, build wireframes and prototypes in Figma and make sure every screen looks and feels right.

Design + artificial intelligence

More possibilities, my own direction.

I use AI tools to explore more ideas in less time, create images and video, and build prototypes. On web projects they also help me structure and develop the site from my design proposal.

That’s how I worked on Ahead, La Colombia Real for Mauricio Lizcano, Colonia 722 and Amor a Mordiscos. The idea, the structure and the creative direction are mine; AI helps me explore options and put those decisions into practice.
