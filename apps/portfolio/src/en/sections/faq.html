01Do you work alone or with a team?

I work independently: you talk directly with me from start to finish. If a project needs it, like building a website or a larger video production, I bring in a trusted collaborator and tell you upfront in the proposal.

02How long does a project take?

It depends on the scope. As a reference: a few pieces or a small campaign, 1 to 2 weeks; a brand identity, 4 to 6 weeks; a website, 6 to 10 weeks. The proposal includes a specific timeline.

03How much does it cost?

Every project is quoted based on its scope and deliverables. After a first conversation, I send you a proposal with the price and everything it includes, with no commitment.

04How do payments work?

Usually 50% to start and 50% on delivery. For long projects or monthly collaborations, we agree on payments per stage or per month.

05How many rounds of revisions are included?

Each stage includes two rounds of revisions. If more are needed, we talk about it before moving on, so there are no surprises.

06Do you work with clients outside Bogotá or Colombia?

Yes. I work remotely through video calls, WhatsApp and Figma. Photo and video shoots happen in person in Bogotá; if they’re outside the city, we plan them together.

07What do I need to get started?

An idea of what you want to achieve and when you need it. If you have references, a logo or copy, great; if not, we’ll build them together.

08Do I get the editable files?

You receive the final files ready to use. Editable files and usage rights are defined in the proposal, so you know from the start what’s yours.

09Do you also build the website?

I design the experience and the interface, and I support you through implementation, using development tools or working with a collaborating developer.

10Do you offer monthly collaborations?

Yes. For brands that need ongoing design, we agree on a number of pieces or hours per month.
