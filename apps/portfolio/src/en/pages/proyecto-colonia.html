01

The house

A 19th-century mansion with a new life.

A house with its own identity. A front door to the Ahead ecosystem.

Colonia 722 is a Republican and eclectic-style house in downtown Bogotá, with a naturally lit courtyard, shared spaces and five restaurants along its facade. The challenge: tell that story and make it easy to go from getting to know the spaces to checking availability within the Ahead ecosystem.

02

The site

Telling the house’s story, section by section.

01 / Story

The house comes first.

Photos of the house, editorial copy and a carousel of details that introduce the house before the rate.

02 / Spaces

Double height, on display.

Studio apartments with large photos and visible amenities; shared spaces organized in tabs, and booking links throughout the journey.

03 / Surroundings

Downtown as a selling point.

Food, location and nearby landmarks so guests can picture their whole stay.

Real screenshots of coloniasuites.com · November 2026. Shown in browser and mobile frames. The availability search continues in Ahead’s booking engine on Stays.

04

System

A template for every partner building.

The name, photos and copy change. The connection to Ahead stays.

The “Ahead sites” template brings together logos for different backgrounds, buttons, links, a search bar for desktop and mobile, testimonials and the “Operated by Ahead” seal. For a new building, you duplicate the frames and switch the color mode; the structure, the search bar and the direct-booking band stay the same.
