01

Context

It’s not a single voice. It’s an ecosystem.

Brands, talent and ideas that move forward when they connect, push against each other and challenge each other.

In a market where everyone knows how to do things well, the difference is in how you think. Breakers Fest was born so creativity could be the force that breaks inertia and helps people reinvent themselves. That’s why it isn’t just another event: it’s a breaking point.
