The idea

What if a simple bite could tell a story?

Every bite is unique: it reflects an emotion, a moment or a memory.

Amor a Mordiscos starts from that observation and turns an everyday gesture into a language of its own: a visual and narrative platform that lets Bimbo connect through emotion, not just through the product.
