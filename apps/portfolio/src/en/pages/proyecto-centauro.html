01

The challenge

Two businesses, one identity.

Clínicas and Seguros spoke to different audiences, with different goals.

The project produced a brand manual with a clear structure for both segments: unified visual and tone-of-voice criteria across every platform, without losing what makes each one different.

The result is a guide that organizes and brings to life a clearer, more human and more strategic language, designed as a practical tool for creative and communications teams.

02

Segments

Warm in Clínicas. Trustworthy in Seguros.

01 / Clínicas

The brand’s most human side.

Health, well-being and closeness with patients. A warm, kind and positive tone to connect with people.

02 / Seguros

The institutional side.

Plans, coverage and benefits, communicated clearly. A professional, direct tone that builds trust.

03

Visual language

Fragments that inspire. Photos that move.

The butterfly wing from the logo becomes a graphic device.

Fragmented or as an outline, it adds movement and cohesion. The photography looks for spontaneous smiles and real moments; the Clan Pro type family keeps everything legible in digital and print.
