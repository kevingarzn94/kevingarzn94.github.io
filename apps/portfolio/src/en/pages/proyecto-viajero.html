The idea

The whole country, in one app.

Tourism, culture, food, places to stay and routes to plan the best trip.

Viajero Co brings together everything a traveler needs to discover Colombia: destinations, plans, a community and a color map of their routes. The goal was to spark interest in tourism, give concrete information for each place and make it easy for everyone to plan their trip.

Structure first; style second.

The wireframes organized the information before giving it visual form. A simple system —Montserrat in three weights and a warm palette of yellows, reds and blues— turned the concept into mockups that were tested and refined with users.
