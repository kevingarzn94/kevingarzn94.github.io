01

The challenge

Joining the conversation of thousands of Colombians.

Six months of graphic pieces, videos, motion graphics and digital content.

The goal was to raise Mauricio Lizcano’s profile, communicate his proposals and bring his vision for the country to new audiences, with a visual language that was distinctive and consistent.

02

Concept

More human. More approachable.

Connecting the campaign with the everyday reality of Colombians.

Not through traditional politics, but through people: the families, workers, students and communities who live the country’s challenges every day.

03

Channels

One voice across social, web and video.

01 / Social

Daily content.

A flexible system for proposals, announcements, daily content and campaign messages under one identity.

02 / Web

Digital platform.

A redesigned site to present his track record, proposals and vision for the country, and to open a direct channel with citizens.

03 / Video

The campaign in motion.

Motion graphics, social content, on-the-ground pieces and audiovisual formats to inform, inspire and connect.
