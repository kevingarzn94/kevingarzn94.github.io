The beginning

One celebration. A world of its own.

A Save the Date: the pre-invitation that announced the wedding.

This isn’t the proposal: that had already happened. Michelle and Felipe are the thread running through a session designed to announce the date. The project connects the Save the Date session, the video, the digital invitation and the pieces for the big day: one story that changes format without losing its character.

Save the Date · the film

The story moves too.

On video, the session finds a different rhythm. The Save the Date brings the couple, the city and the announcement of the celebration together in one piece made to share.

Photography and video production: Kevin Garzón.

04

Concept · visual language

Designing what you want to feel.

One point of view, from the announcement to the details.

The moodboard brings together references, typefaces and a restrained palette. The photography brings the character; the editorial design organizes the information and leaves room for emotion. Art direction shared with Paula Marín, who was responsible for the visual design.

05

Digital experience

An invitation to explore.

From watching the story to being part of it.

The invitation combines photos with the celebration details and the RSVP. The Figma design lets you click through the proposal; the boards show how it was adapted to mobile and desktop. Visual design: Paula Marín. Web development: Felipe Pardo.
