01

Understand · the starting point

Before changing the brand, understand what drives it.

Ahead had grown. Its brand needed to live up to that growth.

The process started by listening: the brand’s history, purpose, audiences, digital assets and tensions. The opportunity was to bring together the trust the operation had earned with a more creative, human and contemporary outlook.

Two ideas became the common thread: creativity, to discover possibilities, and commitment, to keep that trust.

For owners and investors

Peace of mind.
Vision.
Trust.

Explain the value of transforming, operating and growing a property.

For guests

Warmth.
Design.
Experience.

Convey a hospitality you can feel at every touchpoint.

Explore widely. Choose with intent.

The logo was a consequence. The direction started with an idea.

Five territories opened up possibilities around evolution, trust, hospitality, community and lifestyle. The chosen direction brought together the home —the feeling of shelter— with expansion, movement and leadership.

Launch · check-out check-in

A new chapter gets announced. And lived.

Say goodbye to one chapter. Build anticipation. Reveal what’s next.

The check-out / check-in narrative turned the identity change into a story: carousels, stories and an invitation to Expohost set up the transition, and the reveal introduced the new language in motion.

05

Digital · the ecosystem

Designing trust also means designing how people navigate.

One brand. A whole ecosystem.

The brand website and the booking engine do different jobs. The first presents what Ahead offers and guides guests and owners; the second handles the search for stays and availability, built on Stays.
